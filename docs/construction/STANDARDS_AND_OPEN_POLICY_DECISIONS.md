# Construction Drawing E — Standards applicability, document control and decisions requiring PDI approval

## E1. 'Building code' versus 'finish specifications'

Standards inform processes and document requirements; PDI's actual product drawings, released specs, contract requirements and authorized business policy determine acceptance. Do not invent an acceptance tolerance, inspection sampling plan, material specification, coated finish, authority policy, supplier audit interval or ISO certification claim because a standard name exists.

### Applicable standards map (descriptive, not a purchased standards library)

| Area | Framework/source to consider | System-level design effect | What remains PDI- or contract-specific |
|---|---|---|---|
| QMS governance | ISO 9001, applicable controlled edition | Document control, traceable outputs, supplier control, risk-based treatment, change management, corrective action, review evidence | Actual processes, owners, scope, certification, retention and policies |
| Process auditing | ISO 19011 as guidance where useful | Audit-ready evidence/records and later audit module | Audit frequency/method, qualified auditor, formal audits are later phase |
| Measurement management | ISO 10012 and/or relevant calibration standards as chosen | Capture device/method/calibration reference when required | Specific instruments, calibration intervals and uncertainty rules |
| Drawings/GD&T | ASME Y14.5 and/or applicable ISO GPS standards specified on drawing | Technical definition stores feature, tolerance, units, datum, revision, inspection method | Actual dimensions/tolerances/drawing issue and interpretation authority |
| Material/testing | ASTM, SAE, ISO material and test methods cited in PDI specification | Record applicable standard number/edition/test method, measured value and evidence | Specific alloy, treatment, procedure, pass/fail acceptance |
| Sampling | ISO 2859 or other approved plans where applicable | Store lot, sample, plan/version, defects, AQL selection and source | No sampling size/AQL to be autogenerated until policy approved |
| Information security | Recognized security best practice; ISO/IEC 27001 as reference if needed | Access, secrets, audit, recoverability and incident handling | Certification or security framework adoption |

Do not reproduce copyrighted standards text. Store standard ID/edition applicability in controlled technical documents. If a published edition has changed, verify edition before adopting it as contract requirement. An approved drawing overrides a guess based on historical measurements.

## E2. Explicit decisions PDI must sign (unresolved by historical chat)

1. Exact formal authority grants for Engineering release, site qualification, product-source approval, concessions and disposition by risk, escalation/closure and SCAR effectiveness. Seven people may hold multiple grants but may not approve their own work where independence is mandatory; legitimate exception policy requires documented alternate reviewer/cosigner.
2. Product technical acceptance ranges, units, methods, dimensional/vacuum limits, graphical condition standards, CTQ classification and revision/effectivity for each part/source (PDI-IQI forms are questions, not complete specification authority).
3. Receipt sampling plan, lot-size handling, unit traceability, acceptable use-as-is, release if technical baseline missing, status of previously sourced unqualified catalog parts, etc.
4. Risk classifications, when a supplier-site needs assessment, FAI/test/PPAP evidence, SCAR issue and escalation, source suspension and reinstatement criteria, supplier response and verification due dates.
5. Concession quantities and expiration, stock/lot containment policies, partial release, physical verification roles and impact of NCR/SCAR on unrelated sources.
6. Effective dates for change, handling of old lots and field returns, supplier process-change authorization and requalification.
7. Approval/report signature format, PDI brand master artwork/fonts/colors, document numbering, type catalogue, public/supplier-facing evidence redaction, retention and legal hold.
8. Injector bench source formats, authoritative pass/fail rules, diagnostic measurements/units, retest grouping, export destinations and protocol/SSO integration.
9. Production budget/Render resource sizing, monitoring ownership, recovery objectives (RPO/RTO), offsite backup location, approved data migration, user onboarding/MFA and launch gate.
10. Whether historical samples may be de-identified/imported (authorization incl photographs). An old PASS/ACCEPTED in an attached PDF is not new system material authorization.

Treat all listed items as `POLICY OPEN` and store owner/decision/date/status/effective revision in a visible project register. **Do not block development of synthetic workflows** for noncritical open policies: safe illustration may be used in staging, explicitly synthetic and barred from real release. **Do block any consequential production use** lacking relevant approved policy.

## E3. Controlled-record register approach

Appendix B is normative for named candidate record types and links (supplier intake/evaluation, ENG definition/change, FAI/tests, source approval, operating quality, supplier corrective action, supplier declarations and administrative governance). Keep one record/document metadata system with typed sections and PDF output, not 37 separate bespoke static sites. All generated records have: document type/number, revision/edition, subject/source, preparer/approver, issuance date, effective scope, status, related evidence hashes, revision history and PDI branding. Support file attachments for signed third-party PDFs and native structured data entry where PDI owns the form. Applicability checklist is risk/pathway based. Avoid universal PPAP burden on every low-risk B-path source.
